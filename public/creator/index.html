Who

I'm Jayson Frascatore. I live in Kissimmee, Florida. I'm a writer first. I'm also a data scientist, a quality assurance analyst and a project manager. I built Keys to the Race myself, in Python and HTML.

I'm self taught. I didn't go to college. Everything I know about polling, forecasting and code, I learned by doing the work and checking it twice.

Work

Decision Desk HQ

From 2023 to 2025, I was the QA Team Lead and a Project Manager at Decision Desk HQ. I worked alongside The Hill and NewsNation. I sourced the polls for The Hill and DDHQ polling average. I kept the polling averages updated and checked the forecast models those newsrooms relied on.

I worked election night for NewsNation. I ran quality assurance on more than 1,200 candidate images for their coverage. I wrote a QA report every week and built databases that tracked how our accuracy improved over time.

Mercor

In 2026, I worked with Mercor as a political risk analysis and forecasting expert. I wrote long reports on elections and how they changed over time.

Campaigns

In 2022, I was a press and media intern for State Sen. Annette Taddeo. I reported to her press secretary and worked on communications, social media and fundraising. I also wrote debate prep scripts for Charlie Crist's campaign for governor.

Writing
