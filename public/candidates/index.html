Announcing

Everyone starts at a 0.80 weight. Declaring lifts a candidate to 1.00, so their score jumps on launch day. Exploring is 0.90; ruling out a run drops to 0.15.

When news hits

A big moment (a win, a scandal, a viral debate night) adds or takes strength. The effect fades by half every 10 days unless something keeps it alive.

The model

How a score is made.

The Index works like a forecast model. Five inputs are scored as each candidate's share of their party, blended by weight, then cut by scandal drag.

Phase 1 weights, through December 2027. Until the other trackers open, polling carries the whole base. Later, nomination odds come from the Index: the higher the score, the steeper the odds climb.
