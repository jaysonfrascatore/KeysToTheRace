How the average works

One vote per pollster

Each pollster's most recent poll from the last 30 days counts once, so a pollster that releases every week can't swamp the average. Confidence is the total weight behind a number: under 1.0 is low, 1.0 to 2.0 is medium, 2.0 or more is high.

Primary voters only

Primary questions are asked of each party's voters, a smaller group than the full sample. The real margin of error on these numbers is wider than the poll's headline figure.

Rounded figures

Numbers marked with * were only published rounded to the nearest point. They're shown as published. Bars for both parties use the same scale.
